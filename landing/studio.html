Studio.

Chat with Nemotron, then ask it for an image, a short video or a music loop. Everything runs on the free NVIDIA models behind cepheus.codes. No account, no key.

Studio

What should we make?

Auto mode works out whether you want an answer, an image, a video or a song. Try one:

Enter to send · Shift+Enter for a new line

Images

FLUX.2 klein on NVIDIA draws the picture. Nemotron first rewrites your request into a detailed prompt. Downloads as JPEG.

Videos

Nemotron writes a four-shot plan, FLUX draws each shot, and your browser animates them with captions and a soundtrack, then records about 25 seconds of video.

Music

Nemotron composes the chords, melody and groove. Your browser plays them on a built-in synthesizer and exports a WAV file. Instrumental only.

Fair use

Free for everyone, so there are limits: 20 messages a minute, and 6 images a minute or 60 a day per visitor (a video uses 4). Nothing you type is stored.
